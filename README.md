# Essence Wholesale Foods

A responsive, buildless website for Essence Wholesale Foods, Unit 28, Enterprise Park Industrial Estate, Woodbourn Road, Sheffield S9 3JL, United Kingdom.

Run `npm run dev` to preview at http://127.0.0.1:4173. Run `npm run check` for JavaScript syntax checks. The deployable website is in `dist/` and requires no dependencies or build step.

Includes 12 ingredient discovery guides, category and cooking-inspiration filters, alias-aware search, three meal-inspired enquiry lists, a wholesale planner, per-item quantities, eight customer FAQs, a device-local enquiry list, text export, clipboard copy, WhatsApp draft handoff, tap-to-call, mobile navigation and a fixed mobile contact bar. Enquiries are not submitted to a server, and no orders or payments are processed.

Enquiry data is stored in localStorage under `essence-enquiry-v2`. Previous lists migrate from `essence-enquiry-v1`. Quantities, shopping purpose, notes and the wholesale brief remain on the visitor's device until they choose to open a WhatsApp draft or export them. “Clear list & notes” removes the saved content. WhatsApp messages must be reviewed and sent by the visitor.

Product categories and stock photography are illustrative, awaiting owner confirmation. Business phone / WhatsApp: +44 7402 342704. Add verified product details, prices, email and opening hours before a customer-facing launch. No delivery promises, testimonials, stock counts or prices are invented.

Photography: Unsplash and Wikimedia Commons. Assets are downloaded into `dist/assets/`. Wikimedia attributions, source links and licences appear in the website's Photo credits dialog and [ASSET-CREDITS.md](ASSET-CREDITS.md). Typefaces: DM Sans and Manrope, served by Google Fonts. The location graphic is illustrative, with Google Maps used for real directions. Store structured data includes only the supplied business name, address and telephone.

The discovery, FAQ and meal-planning enhancements draw on the browsing patterns and ingredient topics in the [African Stores UK Leeds guide](https://africanstores.co.uk/african-stores-in-leeds). The content is original and adapted to Essence in Sheffield; no directory ratings, verification badges, inventory claims or other businesses' details have been adopted.
